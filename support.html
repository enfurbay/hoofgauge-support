Take a photo that measures well

  1. Stand the horse square on firm, level ground.
  2. Hold the phone at hoof height, square to the side of the hoof. The level guide on the camera turns green when the phone is upright.
  3. Keep the whole hoof and a stretch of ground in frame; avoid tilting the phone down at the hoof.
  4. For lengths, include a ruler in the same plane as the hoof and use the Scale tool.

Why does the number look grey?

Lines start in the middle of the photo. Until you drag their points onto the hoof (and place the Ground Line for tools measured against it) the number is not a reading, and saving offers to leave those lines out.

How precise is a reading?

Every angle shows its placement resolution, for example “±0.10° per pixel”. Longer lines and a closer photo give a finer resolution. Zoom in, use the magnifier and the quarter-pixel arrows to settle each point. The result still depends on how the photo was taken; a photo from in front or above reads steeper or flatter than a true side view.

Using the sensor gauge

Open Sensor Gauge, tap “Set Ground” with the phone flat on the ground the horse stands on, then rest the phone’s long edge on the hoof wall. Hold still for about a second and the reading is captured. “Capture Again” averages several holds. If readings look offset, run Calibrate in Settings: it measures the phone on a flat surface, then flipped.

Practice without a horse

“Practice Hoof” on the Measure tab opens a bundled hoof photo. “Show an example placement” overlays one reasonable placement of the lines. Settings › Help › “Check with a Known-Angle Drawing” opens a drawing whose wall is exactly 52°, so you can check the tools against a known answer.

Reminders

Each horse has a hoof-care cycle (6 weeks by default). Turn on the reminder on the horse’s page to get one notification on the morning a check is due. Nothing is sent to a server.

Backups and moving to a new device

Records are stored on the device. Settings › Backup writes a single file with all horses, readings and photos; Restore reads it back on any device with Hoof Gauge. Deleting the app deletes its records, so export a backup first.

Purchases

Hoof Gauge is a one-time purchase with no subscription. App Store purchases and refunds are handled by Apple.

Hoof Gauge measures angles and proportions from points you place or from the phone’s tilt sensor. It is a record-keeping aid and does not diagnose or advise on treatment. Confirm anything that matters with a physical gauge and your own judgement.
