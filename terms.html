What the app is for

Hoof Gauge measures angles and proportions from points you place on a photo, or from the device’s tilt sensor, and keeps records of those readings. Results depend on how the photo is taken, where the points are placed and how the device is held. The app is a measuring and record-keeping aid for hoof-care work.
