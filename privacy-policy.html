Your records.
On your device.

This policy explains how Hoof Gauge, developed by Enes Furkan Bayram, handles information in its iPhone and iPad app and on this website. Contact: enfurbay@gmail.com.

What Hoof Gauge collects

Nothing. Hoof Gauge has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network requests. The developer does not receive your horses, readings, photos, notes or settings.

Where your data lives

Horses, readings, photos and settings are stored on your device. They are included in your normal device backup. You can delete any horse or reading in the app; deleting the app deletes its records. Backups you export with Settings › Backup are ordinary files that go wherever you save or share them.

Camera, photos and motion

Camera access is used only to take hoof photos when you tap the camera button. Photo library access uses the system picker, so the app only sees the photo you choose. The motion sensor is read only while the Sensor Gauge screen is open, to compute the tilt angle. None of this leaves the device.

Notifications

Notification permission is requested only if you turn on a hoof-care reminder for a horse. Reminders are scheduled on the device and contain the horse’s name and foot; no server is involved.

Sharing and exports

PDF reports, annotated photos, CSV files and backups are created on the device and handed to the share sheet. What you share and where is your choice; the destination’s own privacy practices apply. Reports and backups contain the horse, owner and barn details you entered, so review them before sharing.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store purchases, ratings and any diagnostics you choose to share through Apple’s systems under its own privacy practices. Hoof Gauge does not receive your payment details.

Support correspondence

If you email us we receive your address and what you include. We use it to answer you, keep it only as long as needed for support or legal obligations, and never use it for advertising. You can ask us to delete it.

This website

This static site is hosted by GitHub Pages without analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Hoof Gauge is a professional tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

We will update this page when practices change and revise the date above. Questions: enfurbay@gmail.com.
