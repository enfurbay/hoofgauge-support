HOOF ANGLE MEASUREMENT · IPHONE & IPAD

The hoof gauge
in your pocket.

Photograph a hoof and place two lines, or rest your iPhone on the hoof wall and let the tilt sensor read the angle. Every reading is saved by horse and foot, ready to compare from visit to visit. Records stay on your device.

How it worksRead the privacy policy

Measure from a photo

Toe, heel and pastern angles, heights, base balance, wall angles, sole proportions and a free angle, with 20× zoom and a pixel magnifier.

Measure with the sensor

Rest the phone on the wall. A steady hold is captured on its own; zero on the ground and average several captures.

Keep the record

Horses, four feet, trend charts, before-and-after comparison, PDF reports, CSV export and a single-file backup.

Hoof Gauge is a measuring and record-keeping aid for farriers, trimmers and horse owners. It does not diagnose or advise on treatment. One purchase, no subscription, no account, no ads.
